Thompson SWE Labs

Small products, built and run by the same engineer who does the client work.

Every lab is a real piece of software with real users and real payments, on the same stack I use for client work: sign-in, billing, an admin dashboard and tests that run on every change. They are how I keep the tools sharp between projects.

The labs

Sale Light

A smart light on your desk that goes green when your Stripe account takes a payment, and red on a refund or a dispute. Connect your Stripe account, pick a LIFX or Govee bulb, choose the colors. Nothing to install and nothing to leave running.

Quant Lab

A research bench for trading strategies: define a strategy, backtest it on daily data with walk-forward testing, train machine-learning models on it, and compare the results side by side. Paper trading only; it places no orders.
