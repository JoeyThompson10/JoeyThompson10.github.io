    How the software store does it

    1. The register rings up a sale. The count for that product drops by one. Nobody walks the aisles with a clipboard.
    2. It keeps up with the pace. Every night it checks how fast each product is selling.
    3. It orders before the shelf empties. When a product will not last until the next delivery, the order goes to the right supplier.

    The hand-counted store

    The owner counts every shelf on Monday morning and orders enough to fill them. Whatever sells out before the next Monday stays empty.

    A made-up store with made-up customers. A real version reads sales from your own register and sends orders the way your suppliers take them: email, a spreadsheet, or their ordering site. The hand count is set at two hours a week. Each replay draws a new two weeks of customers.
