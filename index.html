What you can hire me for

Five kinds of work. The first three are where I have spent most of my time.

Integrations and data feeds

Inbound and outbound feeds between a planning system, your ERP, and whatever sits in between. Sales, inventory, and master data coming in; purchase orders going out. I handle field mapping, preprocessing, batching, and message routing, and I stay through go-live.

Tools: REST APIs, Google Cloud Pub/Sub, RabbitMQ, Postman and Newman

Platform extensions

Custom behavior on enterprise supply chain planning platforms: order enrichment, compliance rules, extended attributes, change-detection handlers. Each one comes with a technical design, documentation, and production support.

Tools: Python, SQL, Velocity templates, Git

Data conversion and reconciliation

Moving a business off a legacy system without losing anything on the way: extraction from Oracle, SQL-to-JSON templating, batched API loads, and comparison reports that show the old and new numbers match across millions of rows.

Tools: Python, Oracle and MySQL, Airflow, pandas, NumPy, DuckDB, Parquet

Web applications

Small products and internal tools built end to end: sign-in, a front end people can use, an API behind it, and the cloud hosting underneath.

Tools: JavaScript, React, AWS Amplify

Machine learning

Well-scoped problems where a model beats a rule, such as forecasting and classification. I am in Georgia Tech's M.S. in Computer Science program, specializing in machine learning.

Tools: Python, PyTorch, NumPy

Ways to hire Thompson SWE

Four arrangements. Pick the one your purchasing process already understands.

Contract through your firm

If you staff through an agency or a systems integrator, Thompson SWE signs on corp-to-corp as the vendor. You get a named engineer, not a bench.

Fixed-price project

A written scope, a price, and a delivery date agreed before work starts. Suits a single integration, a data conversion, or a small application.

Subcontractor to a prime

A veteran-owned small business for your team on commercial or federal work, bringing supply chain systems and data engineering. Ask me for current registration and certification status.

Hourly advisory

A design review, a second opinion on an integration, or help with a problem that will not go away. Billed by the hour.
