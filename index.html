What I can do for your business

Most jobs come down to one of three things: build a tool, connect two systems, or get a report out without anyone building it by hand. If you are not sure which you need, that is what the free call is for.

Build

Websites

A clear, fast site that says what you do and how to reach you, on your own domain.

Replacing old systems

Moving off the aging database or desktop program without losing your history. I migrate the data and check that it matches.

Automate and analyze

Connecting your systems

Getting the software you already pay for to share data: accounting, point of sale, online store, customer records. Nobody types the same thing twice.

AI automation

Handing repetitive work to software: sorting routine email, pulling details out of invoices and forms, drafting quotes. I use AI where it helps and plain rules where they are enough.

Reports and dashboards

Forecasting and machine learning

    Where I learned it

    My experience comes from very large companies. The problems are the same ones a small business has, with more zeros. These projects were delivered on a software vendor's implementation team, not under the Thompson SWE name. Clients are described, not named.

    A 24-part software rollout for a national pharmaceutical distributor

    I was the technical lead for the custom-built part of a new purchasing and inventory planning system, delivered in three phases. I designed 17 of the 24 pieces and built 6 of them myself, then supported them once they were live.

    I was also responsible for every connection to the client's other systems: sales, inventory, and product data coming in, and purchase orders going out.

    At your size: one person who can plan the tool, build it, connect it to what you already use, and still be there after it goes live.

    Technical lead

    My share

    Purchase orders filled in automatically across 80,000 products

    A service that adds the right product details to every purchase order on its way out, in the background, so the people placing orders never wait on it.

    The same client needed federally controlled products placed on their own purchase orders to meet DEA rules. I built that and tested it end to end before it went live.

    At your size: orders that fill themselves in, and rules that keep the wrong item off the wrong order.

    Products covered
    About 80,000
    Supplier relationships
    About 60,000

    Moving companies off old systems without losing data

    A tool that pulls data out of a legacy database, reshapes it, and loads it into the new system in batches, later set up to run on a schedule. Alongside it, a tool that compares millions of rows between old and new so a switchover can be checked instead of trusted.

    At your size: moving off the old program with proof that every customer, part, and invoice made it across.

    Used on
